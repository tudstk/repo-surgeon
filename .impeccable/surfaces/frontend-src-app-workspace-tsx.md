# Workspace and identity

Operate, code-led replacement covering login, callback, profile, workspace and unavailable routes.
The public-GitHub-first developer selects an assigned repository and session, runs a demo inspection and reviews saved evidence.
Three desktop areas, no fourth navigation rail; on phones the conversation leads and side content opens in accessible drawers.
No unresolved product decisions; actual user desktop dimensions are unknown; standard desktop and phone viewports provide coverage.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the relationship between selected repository, latest exchange and saved evidence immediately legible, without dashboard metrics.

OWN-WORLD: DeepSource-informed neutral charcoal, quiet rules, system sans, compact readable rows, restrained teal selection and actions, semantic amber/red outcomes; original icons and identity.

STORY: Identify an account, understand assigned-repository and demo-provider limits, choose a session, inspect its actual saved outcome.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 56px shared header and context row anchor three aligned panes: 280px repositories/sessions (260px at 1280px), fluid conversation with sticky composer, 420px event evidence. Plain fixed dividers have no resize affordance. At 1100px and below the conversation leads with two labelled drawer triggers. Teal selected rows connect context.

FORM: Pinned DeepSource Operate direction; no tournament or comp. seed: none (unavailable). System sans serves sustained desktop work under subdued room light; intentional light tokens follow OS preference.

FINISH: Direct solo source and rendered review, package quality checks, DESIGN.md and the design sidecar. Review captures use synthetic fixtures; no raster assets ship in the product.
